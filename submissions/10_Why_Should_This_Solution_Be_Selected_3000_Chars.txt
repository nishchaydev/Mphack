PARIKSHAK-AI is designed as an enterprise cognitive microservice that bolts directly onto MPOnline's existing ASP.NET Core and Oracle 19c examination portal at the MP State Data Centre (SDC) in Bhopal. It addresses the structural operational failures across MP's 94 universities and 28 Lakh students without requiring an overhaul of existing ERP investments.

1. Built for MP's Operating Environment:
State evaluation in MP processes over 3 Crore answer sheets annually. Examiners receive ₹12 to ₹20 per booklet and face quotas that force 3-minute glance-checking on 36-page scripts. In 2026, Barkatullah University faced a 30% error rate from outsourced data processing, Vikram University issued marksheets with 1604/1600 marks, and DAVV Indore paused its digital evaluation rollout due to spine-cutting and page-swapping risks. Generic LLM wrappers fail here because 60%+ of MP students write in Hindi and mixed Hinglish, where traditional OCR engines drop below 50% accuracy.

2. Core Architectural Components:
- Direct Multimodal Evaluation: Eliminates brittle OCR-to-text cascades. Evaluates raw handwriting against official marking schemes, quoting exact student sentences to justify partial marks.
- Non-Destructive Scanning Compatibility: Supports overhead V-cradle scanners to preserve physical thread stitching, eliminating the page-swapping vulnerabilities that halted DAVV's pilot.
- Blind Seed-Script Calibration: Chief Examiners pre-score 5 benchmark anchor scripts. These are randomly injected into examiner batches. If an examiner drifts beyond a 15% tolerance window, the system flags the drift for recalibration before marks are finalized.
- Progressive 3-Tier Velocity Guard: Replaces disruptive screen locks with an adaptive reading floor (T_min). Fast glance-checking triggers soft alerts and mandatory rubric verification, quietly routing persistent speed-checkers to shadow review without provoking teacher union pushback.
- Section 63 BSA Cryptographic Dossier: Generates court-admissible audit PDFs containing student scans, rubrics, dwell-time logs, and SHA-256 hashes, complying directly with the June 2026 MP High Court digital evaluation mandate and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023.

3. Production Feasibility & Economics:
- Non-Invasive Integration: Operates as an asynchronous REST microservice. Ingests scanned pages via temporary pre-signed URLs and returns validated mark arrays to MPOnline's Tabulation Register via Kafka/RabbitMQ queues.
- Unit Economics: At 12,900 tokens per 6-question booklet on Gemini Flash, raw inference is ₹0.16; with caching and SDC overhead, total unit cost is ₹1.14 per booklet.
- Human-in-the-Loop Governance: The AI assists but never commits final marks autonomously, ensuring 100% compliance with UGC guidelines and Ordinance 5.
