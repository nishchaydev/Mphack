PARIKSHAK-AI is an AI copilot for examiners that bolts onto MPOnline's existing exam portal. The AI pre-reads each handwritten answer and suggests marks; the examiner decides every mark. It is built for MP's 94 universities and 27.7 Lakh students, and a working proof of concept runs today.

1. Built for MP's real problems:
MP evaluates over 3 Crore answer booklets a year. Examiners are paid about ₹16–25 per booklet and glance-check 36-page scripts in minutes. At BU Bhopal, around 30% of results from an outsourced agency were suspected wrong, with nearly 10,000 complaints. At DAVV Indore, only about 43% of B.Ed candidates passed before an expert re-evaluation was ordered. Most general-stream students write in Hindi, where text-first OCR pipelines fail.

2. What the system does:
- Reads Hindi and Hinglish handwriting with a multimodal vision model and suggests a mark per rubric criterion, quoting the exact student sentence behind each mark. Quotes not found in the answer, low confidence and hidden instructions such as "give full marks" are flagged.
- Blind seed scripts (Cambridge / IB practice): anchor scripts pre-marked by the Chief Examiner sit unseen in examiner queues; drift beyond 15% is shown on the CoE dashboard and the next scripts go to the Head Examiner.
- Reading-time check: a floor based on each answer's length, equations and diagrams. Rushed marking gets a prompt, then a required check, then a silent second reading. Never a lockout.
- Server-side bounds: a mark above the maximum is rejected before it reaches the tabulation register.
- One-click audit dossier: annotated scans, quoted evidence, reading time, and a signed SHA-256 Merkle root on a hash-chained ledger, supplying the particulars for a Section 63 BSA certificate. Any later edit to stored marks is detected.

3. Proven, feasible and honest:
- Working proof of concept in our repository: examiner workspace with stylus annotation, CoE command centre, audit dossier, tamper detection, and 15 automated tests.
- Bolt-on design: reads scans through pre-signed URLs and returns marks via APIs and queues, with no change to MPOnline's ASP.NET/Oracle systems.
- Estimated AI cost of ₹0.5–3 per booklet at current model prices; our benchmark tool measures real accuracy and cost against teacher marks, and we will present measured results at the finale.
- Human-in-the-loop by design: the AI never commits a mark, keeping legal accountability with the examiner.
